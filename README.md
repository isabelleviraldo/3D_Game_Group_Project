## What We're Making
Fish and Ships is a 3D cooking game set on a ship, inspired by Overcooked. You play as one of the ship's chefs, and your job is to cook specific dishes as orders come in. The catch is that you have to gather your own ingredients first by playing mini-games around the ship, like fishing over the side for the catch of the day. Players move around the deck, collect what each recipe needs, and get dishes out before time runs out.

This is our current plan and it might change as we keep building.
